## Purpose

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") is executed by a corporation
or other legal entity ("Corporation") whose employees or agents contribute to the
Project. It confirms that the Corporation — which ordinarily owns intellectual property
created by its employees in the course of their employment — grants the Project Owner

Where an individual contributes on their own behalf and not in the course of employment,
the [Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) applies instead. Both may apply
to the same person in different capacities.

"Corporation" means the legal entity identified in the signature block below, together
with all other entities that Control, are Controlled by, or are under common Control
with that entity. "Control" means (i) the power, direct or indirect, to cause the
direction or management of such entity, whether by contract or otherwise, or (ii)
ownership of fifty percent (50%) or more of the outstanding shares, or (iii) beneficial
ownership of such entity.

"Project" means any software project made available by the Project Owner in a
source-code repository that references this Agreement, together with the associated
documentation, in each case as it exists from time to time.

"Contribution" means any original work of authorship, including any modifications or
additions to an existing work, that is intentionally submitted by the Corporation —
including by any employee, contractor, or agent acting within the scope of their
engagement — to the Project Owner for inclusion in, or documentation of, the Project.
"Submitted" means any form of electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to the
Project Owner or its representatives, including communication on electronic mailing
lists, source-code control systems, and issue-tracking systems managed by or on behalf
of the Project Owner for the purpose of discussing and improving the Project, but
excluding communication conspicuously marked or otherwise designated in writing as "Not
a Contribution."

The Corporation shall provide, and keep reasonably current, a list of the individuals
authorized to submit Contributions on its behalf (Schedule A). The Corporation may

contact address published in the Project's documentation.

Contributions submitted by an individual not listed on Schedule A, but made within the
scope of that individual's employment or engagement by the Corporation, are nonetheless
covered by this Agreement. Schedule A is an administrative convenience and not a
condition of the license grant.

## 7. Relationship to Individual Contributor License Agreements

Designated individuals, and other individuals contributing within the scope of their
employment or engagement by the Corporation, are not required to separately execute an
Individual Contributor License Agreement for Contributions covered by this Agreement.
For the avoidance of doubt: (a) if an individual ceases to be authorized by the
Corporation to contribute on its behalf (whether due to termination of employment,
change in role, or otherwise), any Contribution submitted by that individual prior to
such cessation remains subject to this Agreement; and (b) if an individual who is or was
authorized by the Corporation to contribute on its behalf wishes to submit a
Contribution in a personal capacity, unrelated to their work for the Corporation, such
Contribution is not covered by this Agreement and requires a separately executed
[Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).
